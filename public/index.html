A Letter from Our Founder
"I built College Shepherd for one very personal reason: my daughter, Bella."

As a sophomore in high school, Bella had just begun her college search, and like most families, we stepped into the process expecting clarity, guidance, and trustworthy information. Instead, what we found was fragmented, outdated, and often biased. There was no true "one-stop" destination — no place that combined real-time data, meaningful personalization, and a genuinely neutral perspective.

We wanted more than just lists and rankings. We wanted a platform that could deeply understand Bella — her goals, her strengths, her interests — and match her with schools where she would truly thrive.

As a family living in Washington State, we're fortunate to have strong in-state options. But Bella has always been clear about one thing — she wants to go out of state and experience something new. Beyond what we already knew, the other 49 states felt like a complete unknown. College Shepherd was built to remove that uncertainty.

With over 30 years in executive search, I've spent my career focused on precision, speed, and high-level matchmaking — connecting the right people with the right opportunities. That same philosophy is at the heart of College Shepherd. Finding the right college should be approached with the same level of care, accuracy, and personalization.

There are over 4,000 four-year colleges in the United States, and every single one offers something different. Choosing the right one is one of the most significant investments a family will ever make — not just financially, but in terms of personal growth, opportunity, and life direction.

And most importantly — I wanted to build something that leaves no kid behind. For any family that cannot afford the platform, a free access link will be made available through their high school college counselor. Every student deserves access to the tools and insights needed to find the right path forward.

College Shepherd was built for Bella — but it exists for every student ready to take that next step.

JH
Jim Herd
Founder, CollegeShepherd  ·  Shepherd Ventures LLC
